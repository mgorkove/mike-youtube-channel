Generate ONE YouTube title for a family revenge / family drama story channel. The audience is older men (50+).

Topic: "[TOPIC]"

WHAT THIS CHANNEL IS ABOUT:
An older man — father, husband, grandfather, father-in-law — is disrespected, betrayed, or taken advantage of by family members. He stays calm, plans carefully, and delivers devastating justice. The satisfaction comes from a patient man who finally says "enough."

STRICT TITLE FORMULA — every title MUST follow this two-part structure:

    [Family Member] [Disrespectful Action], [Narrator's Revenge/Reveal] [Their Reaction]!

Part 1 — The family member's disrespectful action:
- Start with the ROLE: "My Son", "My Daughter", "My Wife", "Son-in-Law", "Daughter-in-Law", "My Kids", "My Brother", "My Sister", "My Ex-Wife", "Ungrateful Son", "Entitled Daughter", "Greedy Brother"
- Use vivid ACTION verbs: "Demanded", "Kicked Me Out", "Took", "Sold", "Changed the Locks", "Put Me in a Home", "Stole", "Said", "Laughed At", "Tried to Take", "Dumped Me", "Cut Me Off"
- Often includes quoted insults in single quotes: 'You're Just an Old Man', 'Sign Over the House', 'We Don't Need You Anymore', 'You Owe Us', 'Dad's Money Is OUR Money'

Part 2 — The narrator's calculated revenge + their reaction:
- BEST reaction words: INSTANTLY REGRETTED, REGRETTED, PANICKED, BEGGED, SPEECHLESS, NEVER FORGOT, CRUMBLED, BACKFIRED, SHOCKED, SCREAMED, BROKE DOWN
- Frame the revenge as calm and calculated: "My Lawyer's Letter", "My Will Reading", "The Truth Came Out", "I Showed Them THIS", "Then the Judge Ruled", "My Accountant Found", "What I Did Next"
- The satisfaction is in the quiet, methodical revenge — NOT hot-headed rage

HIGH-PERFORMING TITLE PATTERNS:
- "My Son Demanded I 'Sign Over the House', My Lawyer's Letter Made Him PANIC!"
- "Daughter-in-Law Said 'You're Just an Old Man', My Will Reading Left Her SPEECHLESS!"
- "My Kids Put Me in a Home to Sell My House, They INSTANTLY REGRETTED It!"
- "Son-in-Law Laughed at Me at Thanksgiving, What I Did Next Made Him BEG!"
- "My Wife Said 'I'll Take EVERYTHING in the Divorce', Then the Judge Read THIS!"
- "Ungrateful Son Took My Business, My ONE Phone Call Made Him CRUMBLE!"
- "My Daughter Cut Me Off From My Grandkids, My Response Made Her PANICKED!"
- "Entitled Kids Fought Over My Inheritance, The Will Reading SHOCKED Them All!"
- "My Son Said 'You Owe Me', I Showed Him the RECEIPTS — He Never Forgot!"
- "Son-in-Law Changed the Locks on MY House, My Revenge Was DEVASTATING!"

CAPITALIZATION:
- Title Case as baseline
- Put 2-4 KEY dramatic words in ALL CAPS (sweet spot for clicks)
- "THIS" is almost always in ALL CAPS when used as a cliffhanger pronoun
- Do NOT over-capitalize (5+ caps words hurts performance)

PUNCTUATION:
- End with ! (most common) or ... (for suspense/cliffhanger)
- Use commas to separate the two clauses
- Quoted insults/demands in single quotes

LENGTH: Target 70-80 characters. Under 50 or over 90 both underperform.

NEVER do these:
- Don't use "reddit", "stories", "r/", or subreddit names in the title
- Don't use generic clickbait ("YOU WON'T BELIEVE", "GONE WRONG", "EPIC")
- Don't reference cheating/infidelity/affairs (that's a different channel)
- Don't reference specific years
- Don't use hashtags or emojis
- Don't make it sound like a news headline — it should sound like a STORY teaser

VARY the titles — each title must feel fresh. Rotate family members, situations, and reaction words across videos.

Return ONLY the title text, nothing else. No quotes, no explanation.
