# Thumbnail Generation — Military Equipment Catalog

Generate a YouTube thumbnail for a military equipment catalog video.

## Layout
- Clean, neutral off-white or light beige background (like a studio product shot)
- Single prominent military item (the most recognizable item from the video topic) centered in the frame
- Semi-realistic 3D render style — clean, detailed, studio lighting
- Item should fill about 60% of the frame

## Text Overlay
- Large bold text in the upper portion
- 2-4 words maximum
- Use BLACK text with ONE key word in RED (#CC0000)
- Text should convey the scope: "EVERY TANK", "ALL WEAPONS", "EVERY GUN"
- Military stencil-style font feel

## Style Rules
- NO complex backgrounds, NO dark backgrounds
- NO people or characters
- NO flags, insignias, or logos
- NO gore or graphic content
- Clean, minimal, product-catalog aesthetic
- The item should look like a museum piece or scale model photograph
- High contrast between subject and background

## Output Format
- First line: EXACT_TEXT: [the 2-4 word text overlay]
- Second line: the full image generation prompt as a single paragraph
- Nothing else
