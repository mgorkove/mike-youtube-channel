# Topic Generation — Military Equipment Catalog

Generate unique YouTube video topics for a military equipment catalog channel.

Each topic should follow one of these patterns:
1. Every [Country] [Equipment Type] ever made (e.g., "Every Soviet tank ever built")
2. Every [Manufacturer] weapon ever made (e.g., "Every SIG Sauer weapon ever made")
3. Every [Country] weapon used in [War] (e.g., "Every Japanese weapon used in WW2")
4. Every [Country] [specific vehicle class] ever built (e.g., "Every US Navy destroyer class ever built")

## Guidelines
- Each topic must be specific enough to fill 50-60 individual items
- Cover a diverse range: different countries, different equipment types, different eras
- Mix weapons-focused topics with vehicle-focused topics
- Popular topics include: Soviet/Russian, American, German, Japanese, Israeli, British military equipment
- Equipment types: tanks, fighter jets, bombers, submarines, rifles, pistols, machine guns, armored vehicles, helicopters, warships
- Wars: WW1, WW2, Korean War, Vietnam War, Cold War, Gulf War
- Manufacturers: Kalashnikov, Colt, Heckler & Koch, SIG Sauer, FN Herstal, Beretta, Lockheed Martin, Boeing, General Dynamics

Return each topic as a short phrase — no full sentences needed.
