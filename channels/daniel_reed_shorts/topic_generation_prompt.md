Each "topic" for this channel is a single 60-second Short composed of 28
distinct "perfectly satisfying" PHOTOGRAPHS unified by a loose visual
through-line. The through-line names the *kind* of satisfaction the
viewer gets — geometry, perfect alignment, repetition, surprising
vantage point, perfect cut, color gradient, macro detail, etc. — not a
literal subject. A good topic names that through-line in 4–10 words and
implies many distinct example photos.

The channel's reference is the r/oddlysatisfying aesthetic. Topics
should span the FULL breadth of that subreddit, NOT just architecture
or landscape. Bias for variety across runs.

Examples of good topics:

ARCHITECTURE / LANDSCAPE / VANTAGE POINT
- Vanishing-point corridors from unexpected vantage points
- Aerial patterns of human-made geometry
- Tunnels of nature: paths canopied by branches and blossoms
- Mirrors and water: landscapes reflecting the sky
- Worm's-eye and bird's-eye views of staircases and atriums
- Symmetrical architecture: cathedrals, temples, courtyards dead-on
- Color-block compositions: minimalist scenes reduced to pure hue
- Ground-level corridors: rows of trees, vines, and crops to the horizon
- Perfect facade grids: apartment windows at dusk

PERFECT ALIGNMENT / KNOLLING / ORDER
- Knolling: everyday objects laid flat in a perfect grid
- Things that fit perfectly into other things
- Crayons, pencils, and tools sharpened or arranged in spectrum order
- Stacked, sorted, and color-graded objects on shelves
- Library shelves and bookstores in perfect repetition
- Fresh, untouched supplies: new pencils, sealed packaging, factory rows

PERFECT CUTS / SECTIONS / CROSS-SECTIONS
- Perfectly clean cross-sections of fruit, bread, soap, and stone
- Hydraulic press flattening unusual objects mid-crush
- Knife through butter, kinetic sand, soap, watermelon, layered cake
- Cutaway shots revealing internal symmetry: pomegranate, geode, onion

LIQUID BEHAVIOR / PHYSICS
- Slow-motion liquid pours: honey, paint, milk, coffee
- Coronets, droplets, and ripples on the moment of impact
- Ink dispersing in water, paint mixing, oil-and-water beads
- Water droplets on surfaces: leaves, glass, hydrophobic fabric

NATURE PATTERNS / MACRO
- Fibonacci spirals, fractal repetition in plants and seashells
- Frost and snowflake crystalline geometry, macro
- Salt flats, basalt columns, cracked-mud tiles, dried-lakebed polygons
- Honeycombs, pinecone scales, pineapple skin, sunflower seed centers
- Dewdrops ringing a leaf edge, raindrops on a spider web

FRESH / PRISTINE / UNTOUCHED
- Fresh snow with a single perfect set of tracks
- Fresh-cut grass with crisp mower stripes
- New parking lot lines, tennis-court chalk, freshly painted road markings
- Clean swimming pools, raked sand gardens, untouched beach ripples

GRADIENTS / COLOR
- Sunset gradients reduced to bands of pure color
- Paint-chip gradients, tile glaze gradients, bookspine color fades
- Aerial color-graded farmland: tulip fields, lavender, canola

MICRO-WORLDS / FOOD AESTHETICS
- Top-down food knolling: bento boxes, charcuterie, mise-en-place
- Pristine ice cream scoops, perfectly poured espresso crema, latte art
- Cake layers and pastry cross-sections in geometric tiers

REPETITION / STACKING
- Stadium seats, beach umbrellas, shipping containers, lifeguard towers
- Stacked firewood, stacked pottery, stacked bricks
- Identical hot air balloons drifting in formation

The topic should imply MANY distinct example photographs (28 of them)
that all riff on the same satisfaction mechanism. Generated topics
should rotate across these CATEGORIES, not stay inside one.

Do NOT generate topic ideas that depend on a specific celebrity, news
event, or narrative. Do NOT name copyrighted landmarks (Eiffel Tower,
Big Ben, Burj Khalifa, etc.).
