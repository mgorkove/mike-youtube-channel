Each "topic" for this channel is a single Short consisting of around 29
distinct "perfectly satisfying" photographs unified by a loose visual
through-line. The throughline is geometry, perspective, repetition, or
vantage point — NOT a literal subject. A good topic names that
through-line in 4–10 words.

Examples of good topics:
- Vanishing-point corridors from unexpected vantage points
- Aerial patterns of human-made geometry
- Tunnels of nature: paths canopied by branches and blossoms
- Mirrors and water: landscapes reflecting the sky
- Stacked, repeated, and nested geometry in cities
- Perfect grids: facades, windows, and patterns of light
- Worm's-eye and bird's-eye views of staircases and atriums
- Color-block compositions: minimalist scenes reduced to pure hue
- Symmetrical architecture: cathedrals, temples, and atriums dead-on
- Ground-level corridors: rows of trees, vines, and crops to the horizon

Generate topics that bias toward THESE archetypes — they all share the
quality of "perspective is the subject." Do NOT generate topic ideas
that depend on a specific celebrity, news event, or narrative.
