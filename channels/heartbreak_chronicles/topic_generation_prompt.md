CATEGORY 1: AFFAIR WITH SOMEONE CLOSE (highest engagement — double betrayal)

Example angles:

- Wife's affair partner turns out to be husband's own brother — family dinner becomes a nightmare
- Husband catches wife with the neighbor after noticing her "evening walks" always go the same direction

- Husband's best man at the wedding is now sleeping with his wife — groomsmen group chat exposes it
- Wife's "gym buddy" is actually husband's coworker — he sees them together at a work event
